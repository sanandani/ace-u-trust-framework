Synthetic data only. This implementation follows the conceptual paper's Section VI architecture and evaluation pathway. It has not been empirically validated and never uses real people's profiles.

Abstract

ACE-U represents professional identity through Authenticity, Credibility, Empathy, and Uniqueness. This repository provides a small, inspectable pathway from synthetic signals through dimension scores and explanations to multi-objective ranking and an offline evaluation comparison.

Four dimensions

Authenticity

Coherence between expressed identity and observable work over time

Signals: Skill and role consistency, overlap of stated skills with authored artifact skills; unsupported shifts are flagged for human review

Credibility

Contextual competence and reliability, distinguishing verified and inferred evidence

Signals: Verified credential/contribution strengths, inferred endorsements, contextual relevance and uncertainty

Empathy

Orientation toward peers and community

Signals: Interaction quality, reciprocity and diversity of interaction types; raw counts are not a proxy

Uniqueness

Contextual differentiation, bounded by trust

Signals: Skill-combination rarity and complementarity; gated by authenticity, credibility and empathy
